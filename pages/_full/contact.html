What's useful to have ready?

None of this is required — a one-line "we're thinking about Alaska next summer" is a perfectly good place to start. But if you have answers to these, the first conversation goes much further:

  • Roughly where, or roughly what kind of trip — cruise, resort, guided tour, still deciding.
  • Roughly when, and how flexible those dates are. Flexibility is often worth real money.
  • Who's going — how many adults, any children and their ages, whether you need rooms near each other.
  • A budget range. Not to spend it, but so I don't waste your time proposing the wrong tier in either direction.
  • Anything non-negotiable — accessibility needs, dietary requirements, a direct flight, no sea days.
