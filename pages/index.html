Why use an advisor at all

Three things a booking site can't do

Perks you can't add yourself

Through Signature Travel Network I can attach resort credits, room upgrades where they're available, and onboard credit on many sailings — on the same published rate you'd pay booking direct.

A person when it goes wrong

Flight cancelled, transfer never showed, wrong room at check-in. You call me instead of joining a queue or waiting on hold with an offshore call centre.

No cost, in most cases

On the great majority of cruise, resort, and tour bookings I'm paid a commission by the supplier after you travel. If a trip ever needs a planning fee, you'll know the number before you commit.

How it works

Four steps, and I do most of them

  1. Consultation

    A free call about where you want to go, who's coming, and how you actually like to travel. No obligation and no script.

  2. Proposal

    Two or three real options with what each one includes, what it costs, and why I picked it. Not forty tabs of search results.

  3. Booking

    I handle the reservation, the deposits, the payment schedule, and the perks. You get a full itinerary before you leave.

  4. Support

    You travel with my number in your phone. Anything goes sideways, that's my problem to solve, not yours.
